D (Day) Rate 06:00 - 21:00

E (Extra) Rate Festive Hours
Applies 24 Dec (18:00) to 27 Dec (06:00) and 31 Dec (18:00) to 02 Jan (06:00).
• Initial Flag Drop: £5.10 (First 300 yards)
• Running Rate: 30p each next 15 yards (up to 45k yards)
• Long Distance: 30p each next 300 yards

Permitted Additional Charges

• Tunnel & Route Tolls: Added to final fare if incurred.
• Inside Cleaning Fee: Maximum £40.00 for interior fouling.
• Outside Cleaning Fee: Maximum £10.00 for exterior fouling.
• Card Payments: No extra charge allowed for card/electronic payments.

Journeys Ending 4+ Miles Beyond Liverpool Boundary

The driver is not obliged to accept the hiring but if he/she does so the following will apply:

• Either the hirer can agree the fare or rate of fare (such as a fixed fare) or the meter should be used.

Complaints & Feedback

For complaints or feedback, please visit the official Liverpool City Council website. To help ensure a thorough investigation, please provide the driver's badge number and the time of the journey. Make a complaint (Council)
