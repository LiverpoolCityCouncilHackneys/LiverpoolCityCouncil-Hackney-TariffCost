D (Day) Rate 06:00 - 21:00
Applies Monday to Friday during daylight hours.
• Initial Flag Drop: £3.40 (First 300 yards)
• Running Rate: 20p each next 165 yards (up to 45k yards)
• Long Distance: 20p each next 300 yards
• Waiting Time: 20p per 60 seconds

N (Night) Rate 21:00 - 06:00
Applies standard nights when Peak/Extra rates do not apply.
• Initial Flag Drop: £4.25 (First 300 yards)
• Running Rate: 25p each next 165 yards (up to 45k yards)
• Long Distance: 25p each next 300 yards
• Waiting Time: 25p per 60 seconds
Active Now
P (Peak) Rate Weekends & Holidays
Applies Fri 21:00 to Sat 07:00, Sun before 08:00, Easter Sunday, and Bank Holidays.
• Initial Flag Drop: £4.75 (First 300 yards)
• Running Rate: 25p each next 155 yards (up to 45k yards)
• Long Distance: 25p each next 300 yards
• Waiting Time: 25p per 60 seconds
Active Now
E (Extra) Rate Festive Hours
Applies Christmas and New Year period.
• Initial Flag Drop: £5.40 (First 300 yards)
• Running Rate: 30p each next 155 yards (up to 45k yards)
• Long Distance: 30p each next 300 yards
• Waiting Time: 30p per 60 seconds
Extra Charges:
• For each passenger beyond one: 20p
• Luggage, prams, bicycles, etc. (per item): 20p
• Soiling Charge (maximum penalty): £50.00
💡 Passenger Information: Drivers must use the meter for all journeys within the Liverpool boundary line. For long distance trips crossing outside Liverpool, a fare may be agreed before the journey starts.
✍️ Feedback & Complaints:
Always note the Badge Number or Vehicle Registration if you experience an issue. You can report overcharging, card machine refusal, or poor service directly to Liverpool City Council. Submit Council Report
